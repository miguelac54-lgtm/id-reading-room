Clinical ID Cases

Selected published cases with infectious diagnoses. Read the initial framing, pause to reason, then open the original case. Final diagnoses stay hidden unless you choose to reveal them.

How to use a published case: Read its introduction without jumping ahead. State a problem representation, a ranked differential, what would change your mind, and what you would do next. Only then review the discussant's reasoning and the final diagnosis. This website links to the originals; it does not reproduce the copyrighted case narratives.
